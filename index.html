The atelier

How a commission is made.

From first sketch to final polish, four unhurried stages — and you are part of every one of them.

01

Consultation

Sit with a jeweller, not a salesperson. We discuss budget, metal, stone and the story the piece should carry.

02

Sketch & Quote

Hand-drawn renderings and a written, itemised estimate — making charges fixed before any gold is melted.

03

Hand Crafting

Our karigars work the metal at the bench — setting, engraving and polishing in stages you are welcome to watch.

04

Assay & Delivery

Independent hallmarking, certification and a lifetime service file handed over with your piece.
