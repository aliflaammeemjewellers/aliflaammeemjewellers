The House

Three generations,
one standard of honesty.

What began in 1979 as a single counter in the Liberty Market gold souk is today a family workshop and showroom trusted by brides, collectors and investors alike.

Our karigars still set each stone by hand, under the loupe, at the same bench their teacher used. Nothing leaves the vault without a hallmarked assay, a documented weight and a written invoice — because jewellery bought for a lifetime deserves paperwork that lasts just as long.

Weighed in Your Presence

Every gram, every stone and every making charge is shown on a printed bill before purchase.

Bench-Made, Not Machine-Made

Filigree, granulation, kundan and repoussé — techniques that machines cannot fake.

Lifetime Exchange & Buyback

Fair, published rates on old gold — plus complimentary cleaning and re-polishing, forever.

The Atelier

How a commission is made

From first sketch to final polish, four unhurried stages — and you are part of every one of them.

01

Consultation

Sit with a jeweller, not a salesperson. We discuss budget, metal, stone and the story the piece should carry.

02

Sketch & Quote

Hand-drawn renderings and a written, itemised estimate — making charges fixed before any gold is melted.

03

Hand Crafting

Our karigars work the metal at the bench — setting, engraving and polishing in stages you are welcome to watch.

04

Assay & Delivery

Independent hallmarking, certification and a lifetime service file handed over with your piece.
