Our Story

A Legacy of
Trust & Craftsmanship

25+ Years of
Excellence

Founded with a single showcase and a bench of two goldsmiths, Alif Laam Meem Jewellers has grown into a trusted name in fine jewellery across South India.

Our master craftsmen combine traditional goldsmithing with contemporary design, creating pieces that outlast trends. Every jewel is weighed, graded and hallmarked in front of you — no claims, no guesswork, only what you can verify.

Ethical Sourcing

Conflict-free stones and responsibly sourced precious metals, documented at purchase.

Master Craftsmanship

The Atelier

From Sketch to Hallmark

01

Consultation

Share the occasion, budget and references. We advise on metal, stone grade and what will wear well over time.

02

Design & Selection

Choose from the vault or commission a sketch. Stones are shown loose under loupe before anything is set.

03

Handcrafting

Cast, set and polished at our bench — typically three to five weeks, with progress photographs along the way.

04

Certify & Deliver

BIS hallmarking, independent diamond certification, insured delivery and lifetime servicing in writing.
